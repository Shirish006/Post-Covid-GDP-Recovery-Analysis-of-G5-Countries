Recovery Score Composition
Recovery Score  =  (GDP Growth Rate × 0.5)  −  (Inflation Rate × 0.25)  −  (Unemployment Rate × 0.25)
GDP Growth Rate × 0.5 — Primary driver; annual GDP growth rate weighted at 50%. Higher growth lifts the score.
Inflation Rate × 0.25 — Erodes purchasing power; higher inflation drags the score down (25% weight).
Unemployment Rate × 0.25 — Signals labour market weakness; higher unemployment lowers the score (25% weight).
